# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose
Memorial archive and portfolio site for Carlo Chiavegato ("Carolus", 1923–2015), Italian painter and photographer. One static page: hero, painting gallery, photography gallery, biography, critical texts, contacts. Italian language.

## Users
Family, friends, art lovers, galleries, and researchers discovering Carolus' work. Viewed on phones and desktops, often shared via link. Mood: contemplative, respectful, gallery-like.

## Register
brand

## Brand and Tone
Quiet elegance of a fine-art gallery. Cormorant Garamond display (italic as signature beat: Ca*ro*lus), Josefin Sans body. Soft blue accent (`--terra` token), oklch palette, generous whitespace. Motion is slow, dignified, never playful.

## Anti-references
- Flashy portfolio templates, parallax circus, scroll-jacking
- SaaS landing-page tropes (hero metrics, card grids)
- Bounce/elastic easing, fast snappy motion

## Strategic Principles
- The artwork is the protagonist; UI recedes
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere
- No build step; everything stays in site/index.html
- No em dashes in user-facing copy
